💊 Antimicrobial Resistance Genes

Clinical Significance of AMR Genes

  • Vancomycin resistance (vanA, vanB, vanD, vanM): High‑level resistance to last‑resort glycopeptides. VRE is a WHO high priority pathogen.
  • Linezolid resistance (optrA, cfr, poxtA): Compromises last‑line oxazolidinones. Often plasmid‑mediated and co‑spreads with vancomycin resistance.
  • High‑level aminoglycosides (aac, ant, aph): Abolishes synergy with cell‑wall agents, limiting treatment options.
  • Beta‑lactamase (pbp5 alterations): Intrinsic resistance to ampicillin, common in clinical isolates.
  • Other families: Macrolides (erm), tetracyclines (tet), fluoroquinolones (qnr), etc.
